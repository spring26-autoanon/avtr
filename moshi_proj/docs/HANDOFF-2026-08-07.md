### The loss mask — the central idea

Cross-entropy pushes `P(⟨ret⟩)` down at every position of every marker-free window. With ~135
markers against thousands of such windows, that negative signal dominates and un-teaches the
base model's trigger — which is why `RAG_TOKEN_WEIGHT=25` stalled at ~50% firing.

The fix follows from what we actually know: a **labelled clip** has known ground truth
everywhere, so it trains normally; an **unlabelled window** was never verified, so we assert
nothing — the rag logit is blanked from the softmax and gets no gradient. Costs nothing
elsewhere: one logit index in the text head, audio codebooks untouched.

**This unlocked two things.** All 135 min of dialogue stays in Track B unmarked at zero cost
(dialogue mining demoted to optional), and the same audio can appear both unmarked (long-form,
for voice) and marked (clipped, for the trigger) without contradiction — which is what the
tonight plan's two-pass design depends on.

## Findings from this session

**The delay barely engages on the current corpus.** Eq. 3 bounds `d'` by `d_lead`; her leads
are 0.43–1.2 s, so delays are 0.2–0.6 s against a real 1.7–3.4 s serve latency. **0%** of draws
reach it — needs a 3 s lead for 32%, 4 s for 63%. A null delay result in 4a is a *data* limit,
not a broken implementation. The brief now asks for a ~3 s runway; the code fix and the
recording are the same fix from two directions.

**Whisper strips disfluencies.** Zero "um"/"uh"/"hmm" in 5,320 words of retrieval clips. They
never enter the text stream, so they cannot be reinforced by asking for more. Discourse markers
("like" 554, "yeah" 271, "oh" 80) *do* survive and are trained at full weight.

**Register collapse is the real filler problem.** 72 discourse markers per 1k words in casual
dialogue vs **19** in Q&A — she gets 3.7× more formal when answering questions, which is the
mode we train hardest. Almost certainly the "it lost its filler words" complaint.

**Topic coverage in 135 min of dialogue** (this reordered the brief): name **0**, fighting
**0**, plants **0**, tattoos 1, work 7, travel 14, hometown 29, music 29.

**Corpus shape:** `replay/retrieval/` = 78 clips, 68.3 min, 119 markers, 19 clips marker-free.
Answer window after each marker: median 18.7 s, p10 6.3 s — room is not the constraint.
